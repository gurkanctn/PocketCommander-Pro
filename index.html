HELP & INFO

You are the commander. Under continuous attack waves of enemy forces, you must build defenses, train an army, and protect your Town Center at all costs.

Tips:

  • Builders automatically heal at base (Costs Gold).
  • Soldiers regenerate health if out of combat.
  • Use Walls to reroute enemies (Enemies break walls if stuck).
  • The PATH button (bottom bar) shows the enemy walk route and each enemy's live path — great for wall placement planning.
  • Every 10 waves, a Boss appears and a new Gold Mine spawns.
  • Biomes 🌿 Grasslands · 🏜️ Desert · ❄️ Snowfield · 🦟 Swamp — each has themed enemies with different stats (fast scorpions, tanky frostlings, hard-hitting slimes).

INSTALLATION

Add to Home Screen on iOS (Share Btn) or Android (Chrome Menu) for a full app experience.

This is a PWA — after the first load the game is cached by a service worker and plays fully offline.
