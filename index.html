HELP & INFO

You are the commander. Under continuous attack waves of enemy forces, you must build defenses, train an army, and protect your Town Center at all costs.

Tips:

  • Research can add two Builder types at the Town Center: the Hauler (carries ~1.75x the gold, builds slowly) and the Engineer (builds twice as fast, sturdy, carries little). All Builders share one population cap.
  • Builders construct every building you place (more Builders build faster, up to 4 per site) and mine gold when nothing is waiting. Tap a site to cancel it for a full refund.
  • Builders automatically heal at base (Costs Gold).
  • The REPAIR button (bottom left) repairs the Town Center first, then other damaged buildings, for gold. The Town Center also heals slowly by itself, and idle Builders repair it for free. Tap a ruin, or REBUILD, to raise it again for half price.
  • Builders run from armed enemies that come close and go back to work once it is safe (Engineers are braver). They keep their load and unfinished sites.
  • Soldiers regenerate health if out of combat.
  • Use Walls to reroute enemies. If you seal every route, enemies stop at the cheapest wall and break through it.
  • The PATH button (bottom bar) shows the enemy walk route and each enemy's live path — great for wall placement planning.
  • Between waves you get a short build break. ▶ WAVE calls the next wave early (2g per second skipped); ×1/×2/×3 changes game speed.
  • Enemies change as waves go on: fast Swarmer packs, slow Brutes, acid Spitters, Armoured plates (shells pierce them), exploding Bombers, Healers (kill them first), Splitters and Flyers that ignore walls. Each biome has its own boss.
  • Win by clearing wave 50 (the last wave cannot be skipped with ▶ WAVE). You can save a screenshot of the victory, play on in endless mode, and your wins show as 🏆 badges for each biome and map on the main menu and in Achievements.
  • Every 10 waves, a Boss appears and a new Gold Mine spawns.
  • Select a finished tower to upgrade its fire rate, power or health (5 steps each). Once you have researched a tower type the build bar shows one TOWERS ▾ button with a menu of every type; new unlocks pulse with a NEW badge.
  • Build a LAB, select it and open Research… to unlock new towers (gold + time). Unresearched buttons stay hidden; each base upgrade speeds research up by 15%.
  • Research at the Lab unlocks new Barracks units: Rifleman (fast, fragile, cheap), Guardian (tough, draws enemy fire, 2 slots) and Grenadier (slow zone grenades, 2 slots). The Barracks limit is counted in slots (6 per Barracks).
  • Every Soldier is a recruit with its own rolled power, attack speed, health and move speed. A higher Barracks tech level (and the Drill / Elite research) raises the roll; ★ marks Good and ★★ Elite recruits, who also cost more upkeep. Select a unit to read its card.
  • Army units are limited to 6 slots per Barracks; tap SOLDIERS to select them all, then tap the ground. A Barracks can set a rally point for new recruits.
  • Press and hold (or long-click) any unit or building for a quick stats readout. Esc pauses.
  • Your progress is saved when you pause or leave the page — use CONTINUE RUN on the menu. Settings has volume, text size, colour-blind and high-contrast modes.
  • Biomes 🌿 Grasslands · 🏜️ Desert · ❄️ Snowfield · 🦟 Swamp — each has themed enemies with different stats (fast scorpions, tanky frostlings, hard-hitting slimes).
