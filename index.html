HELP & INFO

You are the commander. Under continuous attack waves of enemy forces, you must build defenses, train an army, and protect your Town Center at all costs.

Tips:

  • Builders automatically heal at base (Costs Gold).
  • Soldiers regenerate health if out of combat.
  • Use Walls to reroute enemies (Enemies break walls if stuck).
  • Every 10 waves, a Boss appears and a new Gold Mine spawns.
  • Tap ▮▮ to pause. Tap 🔊 to mute audio.
  • Gold counter turns orange when upkeep is due soon.

LEVEL EDITOR

Paint terrain, place GOLD mines, and set a PATH (first point = enemy spawn, last point = your base). Use ERASE to remove tiles, mines, or waypoints. CLR PATH resets all waypoints.
